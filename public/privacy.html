This notice explains the data used by our browser-based travel poster studio.

Posters and uploads

Names, quotes and selected places are processed in your browser. Uploaded photos are resized and cropped locally; this app does not upload them to a server. Draft settings are stored using localStorage; uploaded photos are not included in saved drafts. Clear a draft with Reset poster & local draft. Share links exclude names, quotes and uploaded photos. City locations are included only when you enable the city-sharing option.

City search and public photographs

City searches are sent to Open-Meteo and use GeoNames data. Searching for photographs sends the selected city or map-pin coordinates to Wikimedia Commons. Selecting an external image downloads it from Wikimedia. These providers receive ordinary request information, such as your IP address. The geographic map, fonts and time-zone lookup are hosted with the site.

Optional Google advertising

Google AdSense is integrated using publisher ca-pub-3728341118830943. Advertising is enabled by default to support the studio. The app does not show an initial advertising-choice popup. You can turn ads off through Ad privacy choices in the footer; any previously saved opt-out is respected, and a Global Privacy Control signal defaults to ads off. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers and process device, IP and interaction data to serve, prevent fraud and measure advertising. We request non-personalized advertising; regional eligibility and consent requirements still apply. Google may show its own consent message. Serving ads also depends on Google approving the site.

Google explains its processing at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and its Privacy Policy. You can change your advertising preference with Ad privacy choices in our footer. Turning advertising off reloads the page to stop loading its code; this does not erase cookies already stored by another provider. Use your browser settings to remove existing cookies.

Local storage and hosting

This app uses localStorage for poster drafts and your advertising preference. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process request logs under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not operate a login, analytics service or database for uploaded memories.

Questions

For questions about this site, contact the owner through the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not post private photos or sensitive information in a public issue.
